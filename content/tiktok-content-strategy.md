# Slam5 TikTok Content Strategy

**Account:** @winclubapp
**Updated:** 2026-04-18

## Current Status
- 10 followers, 9 videos, max 1,308 views
- Problem: every video is a CTA push ("check bio", "take a pill") — TikTok penalizes this
- Bio "Cure ADHD in 90 days" sounds like supplement spam

## Benchmark: @adhdvision
- 1.8M followers, 9.7% engagement rate, avg 362K views/video
- Full analysis: `competitor-analysis-adhdvision.md`

## Rule #1: Value first, CTA never (or last)
TikTok algorithm rewards watch time and saves — not link clicks.
Give value in every video. CTA max once every 5 videos.

## Hashtag Stack (use every video)
```
#adhd #adhdtiktok #adhdawareness #adhdinwomen #adhdsquad #adhdtok #adhdprobs #adhdcheck
```
Max 8 tags. No trending/generic tags (#fyp, #viral, etc.)

## Content Formats (proven in niche)

### Format 1 — "Can you relate?" (highest viral potential)
Hook: relatable ADHD moment, no explanation needed
Example: "Can you relate? Starting 7 tasks and finishing 0 💙"
Post as: text on screen + reaction or b-roll

### Format 2 — Science explanation
Hook: "The real reason you can't start tasks"
Body: 1 actual study or mechanism explained simply
Example: "ADHD brains have 30% less dopamine at baseline. Here's what that means for your motivation 👇"

### Format 3 — Comparison
Hook: "ADHD brain vs normal brain: [scenario]"
Example: "ADHD brain vs normal brain: trying to reply to one email"

### Format 4 — Symptom reveal
Hook: "No one talks about this ADHD symptom"
Body: name a lesser-known symptom, validate it, connect to Slam5

### Format 5 — Series
"ADHD productivity lesson #1" → builds returning viewers
Commit to 10+ episodes minimum

## Posting Cadence
- Minimum: 1 video/day for first 30 days
- Best times: 7-9am, 12-1pm, 7-9pm (local US time)
- Never post 2 CTA videos in a row

## What NOT to do
- No "check my bio" in every video
- No supplement/cure language ("cure ADHD in 90 days" → remove from bio)
- No generic hooks ("Hey guys today I want to talk about...")
- No videos under 15 seconds
