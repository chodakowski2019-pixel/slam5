## Key Takeaways for Slam5 TikTok
- **"Can you relate?" format** = strongest viral trigger in ADHD niche
- **Simple question hook** (2-5 words) outperforms long descriptions
- **Citing science** (real studies/doi) builds authority + increases shareability
- **Series format** (lesson 21, part 2) drives followers to come back
- Niche hashtags are enough — don't dilute with trending tags
- Average 350K+ views per video at 1.8M followers = strong algorithm favor
